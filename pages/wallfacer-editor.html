How to
  • Drag a tile to move it. Dropping it on another tile swaps them.
  • Shift/Ctrl-click or Shift-drag the background to select several; drag or use arrow keys to move them together.
  • Drag the background to pan; scroll to zoom.
  • Double-click an empty cell to add text there.
  • Drop image files from your desktop onto a cell, or paste one with Ctrl+V.
  • Click the dot between two tiles to cut or restore that connection. Cut connections can't be traveled in the viewer.
  • Connect folder → choose wallfacer/ in your local repo clone. Save then renames, adds, and deletes files and writes manifest.json. You still commit them yourself.
